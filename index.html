Recent Trends

ICT · Electronics · Mechanics in Tactical Systems

ICT

Software-Defined Radios (SDR)

Modern forces are rapidly adopting SDRs for flexible waveform support, enabling seamless switching between Link 16, Link 22 and national waveforms.

Electronics

Miniaturised Multi-Band Antennas

New multi-band antenna designs reduce platform signature while supporting simultaneous Link 16, SATCOM and UHF LOS.

Mechanics

Ruggedised Cooling Systems

Advanced heat-pipe and liquid-cooling solutions keep high-power JTIDS/MIDS terminals operational in extreme environments.

ICT

AI-Assisted Network Management

Machine-learning tools now assist operators in predicting congestion and optimising time-slot allocation in Link 16 networks.

Electronics

Next-Gen Crypto Modules

Programmable crypto devices with faster key loading and multi-level security are becoming standard for modern COMSEC.

Mechanics

Modular Integration Kits

Quick-swap mechanical and electrical interface kits allow rapid installation of datalink terminals across platforms.
